## Netflix Content Dashboard (Tableau)

Interactive dashboard analyzing the Netflix Movies and TV Shows catalog: content type distribution, ratings, genres, countries and growth over time.

**Live dashboard:** [Tableau Public link](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shamil.zaripov/viz/Netflix_dashboard_17819417992420/Netflix)

### Data source

Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset (Kaggle), approximately 8,780 titles with fields including type, title, rating, duration, date added, release year, genre and description.

### Tools

Tableau Public.

### Dashboard sections

* **Total movies and TV shows by country.** World map showing content volume by country, from 1 to 3,207 titles.
* **Ratings.** Distribution of titles by content rating (TV-MA, TV-14, TV-PG, R, PG-13 and others).
* **Movies and TV shows distribution.** Overall split between movies and TV shows.
* **Top 10 genres.** Most common genre combinations in the catalog.
* **Total movies and TV shows by year.** Volume of movies vs TV shows added over time, 2008 to 2021.
* **Title detail panel.** Type, title, rating, duration, date added, release year, genre and description for a selected title.

### Key insights

* Movies make up 69.7% of the catalog (6,120 titles) versus 30.3% for TV shows (2,660 titles), so Netflix's library is still primarily film focused rather than series focused.
* TV-MA and TV-14 together account for the large majority of rated titles, meaning the catalog skews toward mature and teen audiences rather than family content.
* Dramas combined with International Movies is the single largest genre group (360 titles), followed closely by Documentaries (334) and Stand-Up Comedy (274), showing Netflix's strategy of pairing drama with international appeal.
* Content additions grew sharply from 2015 and peaked around 2019, then dropped off in the most recent years, likely reflecting incomplete data for those years rather than an actual slowdown.
